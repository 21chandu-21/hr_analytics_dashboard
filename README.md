# HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview
An interactive HR Analytics Dashboard developed using Power BI to explore workforce demographics, employee attrition, salary distribution, job satisfaction, and department-level trends. The dashboard presents key workforce metrics through interactive visualizations and KPI reporting to support data-driven HR analysis.

## Objectives
- Analyze employee demographics and workforce distribution.
- Examine employee attrition across age groups, gender, departments, and job roles.
- Compare salary, job satisfaction, and business travel patterns.
- Present workforce KPIs in an interactive, easy-to-understand dashboard.

## Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI Desktop** — Dashboard development and visualization
- **DAX** — Measures and KPI calculations
- **Power Query** — Data preparation and transformation
- **Data Modeling** — Relationships between tables and interactive filtering

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Workforce Overview
- Employee count, average salary, average age, and monthly salary KPIs
- Department and education-field comparisons
- Salary distribution by age
- Job-role analysis, job satisfaction, and workforce demographics
- Interactive slicers for department, age group, business travel, education field, gender, and job role

### 2. Employee Attrition Analysis
- Overall employee attrition rate
- Attrition analysis by age group and gender
- Department-related and education-field comparisons
- Job-role, business-travel, job-satisfaction, and monthly-income analysis
- Interactive filters to explore workforce patterns
